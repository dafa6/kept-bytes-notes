Kept Bytes

Two notes on the parts of a generation you only miss after they are gone.

About

A short notebook kept by Andy Swift.

I build a third-party browser interface for OpenAI's GPT Image 2.5. The notes here come from the unglamorous half of that work: what to store, what expires, what a vendor did and did not claim, and which of those you will need to answer a question three months from now.

They are general on purpose. Specific figures change, so they live on a page that can be kept current rather than frozen into a post: the measured cost per image at each quality tier is published next to the upstream cost so the margin is checkable.

The interface is not an OpenAI product and is not affiliated with OpenAI. No sponsorship, no affiliate links.
